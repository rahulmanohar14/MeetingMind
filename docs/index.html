🧠 MeetingMind

The AI that remembers what your team forgot it promised

From transcript to decisions, actions, and accountability—without losing context across meetings.

Why it matters

$37B in annual U.S. productivity is lost to poorly run meetings, including unclear outcomes and unowned follow-ups (industry research estimates often cited in enterprise collaboration studies).

Professionals can spend on the order of ~31 hours/month in meetings, yet commitments and open questions are rarely tracked in a single system.

What MeetingMind does

Fine-tuned Classifier (via API)

LLM-backed classification of each utterance into decision, action, question, deferral, or discussion—so the signal survives noisy conversations.

Cross-Meeting Memory

Vector store plus a graph of recurring issues so the same “we’ll circle back” does not reappear in meeting after meeting without visibility.

Decision Decay Model

Confidence, completion risk, and follow-through stats highlight where accountability is most likely to break down before it does.

    ▼

    ▼
[Streamlit app.py]  dashboards, charts, graph viz
